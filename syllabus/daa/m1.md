# Design and Analysis of Algorithm (BCA50113)
## Module 1: Algorithm Development & Complexity Analysis [10H]

### Syllabus Content
* **Asymptotic Complexity**: Details of different asymptotic notations for time and space complexity ($O$, $\Omega$, $\Theta$, $o$, $\omega$), time-space trade-offs, and their mathematical significance.
* **Solving Recurrences**: Substitution method, recurrence tree method, and Master's Theorem.
* **Analysis**: Finding time complexities by writing programs on a computer.

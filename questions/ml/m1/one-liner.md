# One-Liner Questions

1. **Question:** Define Machine Learning.
**Answer:** A subset of AI that uses algorithms to parse data, learn from it, and make informed decisions or predictions without explicit programming.

2. **Question:** State the main difference between AI and ML.
**Answer:** AI is the broader concept of machines executing tasks smartly, while ML is the specific application of machines learning from data.

3. **Question:** What is the fundamental principle of ML?
**Answer:** Algorithms improve their performance on a specific task through experience (data) over time.

4. **Question:** Define Big Data.
**Answer:** Massive, complex datasets that cannot be effectively processed or analyzed using traditional data management tools.

5. **Question:** What is Descriptive Analytics?
**Answer:** The interpretation of historical data to identify trends and understand what happened in the past.

6. **Question:** What is Predictive Analytics?
**Answer:** The use of statistical models and ML techniques to determine the likelihood of future events based on historical data.

7. **Question:** How does ML leverage Big Data?
**Answer:** ML uses the massive volume and variety of Big Data as training material to build highly accurate predictive models.

8. **Question:** Why do we use Python for ML practicals?
**Answer:** Python offers a robust ecosystem of specialized libraries for data manipulation, mathematical computation, and visualization.

9. **Question:** What is the purpose of Data Visualization?
**Answer:** To represent data graphically, making complex patterns, trends, and outliers easy to understand at a glance.

10. **Question:** Name two Python libraries used for data visualization.
**Answer:** Matplotlib and Seaborn.

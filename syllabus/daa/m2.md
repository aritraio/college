# Design and Analysis of Algorithm (BCA50113)
## Module 2: Searching and Sorting Algorithm [10H]

### Syllabus Content
* **Searching Algorithms**: Details of Linear Search and Binary Search (comparisons), Interpolation Search, and their complexity.
* **Sorting Algorithms**: Merge Sort, Quick Sort, and Heap Sort. Complexity analysis in best-case, worst-case, and average-case.

# Software Engineering (BCA50112)
## Module 3: Requirement Analysis and Specifications [10H]

### Syllabus Content
* **Requirements Basics**: System and software requirements, functional and non-functional requirements, domain requirements, and user requirements.
* **Elicitation & Analysis**: Techniques, viewpoints, interviewing, scenarios, and use-cases.
* **Process Modeling & Modeling Tools**: Physical and logical Data Flow Diagrams (DFDs), Entity-Relationship (ER) diagrams, and Data Dictionaries.
* **Requirements Specification**: Feasibility study, requirement validation, Software Requirement Specification (SRS) structure, and contents.

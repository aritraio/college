# Flashcards

**Flashcard 1**
*Front:* Artificial Intelligence (AI)
*Back:* The simulation of human intelligence processes by machines, encompassing reasoning, learning, and self-correction.

**Flashcard 2**
*Front:* Machine Learning (ML)
*Back:* A branch of AI based on the idea that systems can learn from data, identify patterns, and make decisions with minimal human intervention.

**Flashcard 3**
*Front:* Traditional Programming vs. ML
*Back:* Traditional: Data + Rules = Answers. Machine Learning: Data + Answers = Rules.

**Flashcard 4**
*Front:* The 3 Vs of Big Data
*Back:* Volume (amount of data), Velocity (speed of data generation), Variety (different types of data).

**Flashcard 5**
*Front:* Descriptive Analytics
*Back:* Answers the question: "What happened?" Uses data aggregation and mining to provide insight into the past.

**Flashcard 6**
*Front:* Predictive Analytics
*Back:* Answers the question: "What could happen?" Uses statistical models and forecasting techniques to understand the future.

**Flashcard 7**
*Front:* Big Data in ML Context
*Back:* Big Data acts as the "fuel" for ML algorithms. More high-quality data generally leads to better, more accurate ML models.

**Flashcard 8**
*Front:* Data Representation
*Back:* The process of converting raw data into a format (like graphs, charts, or structured arrays) that algorithms and humans can interpret effectively.

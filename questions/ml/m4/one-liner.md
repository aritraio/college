# One-Liner Questions

1. **Question:** What is a Machine Learning Strategy?
**Answer:** The plan connecting technical ML capabilities directly to measurable business outcomes and ROI.

2. **Question:** Define Business Problem in the context of ML.
**Answer:** The specific operational or financial challenge that justifies the investment in an ML solution.

3. **Question:** What is a Pilot Project?
**Answer:** A low-risk, controlled implementation of an ML model designed to prove concept viability before full-scale deployment.

4. **Question:** What is the Best Learning Model?
**Answer:** The algorithm that provides the optimal balance of accuracy, speed, and interpretability for a specific business use case.

5. **Question:** Define Machine Learning Skills.
**Answer:** The combination of data engineering, algorithmic knowledge, and business acumen required to deploy successful models.

6. **Question:** What does the Patient Health Use Case involve?
**Answer:** Utilizing historical medical data to predict patient risks, personalize treatments, or forecast hospital readmissions.

7. **Question:** Describe the Proactive IT Issues Use Case.
**Answer:** Analyzing telemetry and log data to predict and resolve infrastructure failures before they impact end-users.

8. **Question:** What is the Fraud Protection Use Case?
**Answer:** Deploying classification models to flag unusual patterns or unauthorized transactions in real-time.

9. **Question:** What is Model Training?
**Answer:** The computational process of feeding historical data into an algorithm so it can identify patterns and establish parameters.

10. **Question:** What are Prediction Algorithms?
**Answer:** The final, trained models deployed to evaluate new, unseen data and generate actionable forecasts or classifications.

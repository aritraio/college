# One-Liner Questions

1. **Question:** Define Supervised Learning.
**Answer:** A paradigm where the model is trained on a labeled dataset to predict outcomes for unseen data.

2. **Question:** Define Unsupervised Learning.
**Answer:** A paradigm where the model infers patterns, clusters, or structures from unlabeled data.

3. **Question:** Define Reinforcement Learning.
**Answer:** A behavioral learning model where an agent learns to make decisions by performing actions and receiving rewards or penalties.

4. **Question:** What are Neural Networks?
**Answer:** A subset of ML techniques structured in layers of nodes designed to recognize complex patterns, loosely mirroring biological brains.

5. **Question:** What is the role of Statistics in Machine Learning?
**Answer:** It provides the mathematical foundation for estimating relationships, modeling probability, and drawing inferences from data.

6. **Question:** What is the role of Data Mining?
**Answer:** The process of exploring and extracting usable patterns or insights from large datasets, serving as a precursor or companion to ML.

7. **Question:** What is Data Preprocessing?
**Answer:** The critical step of cleaning, transforming, and normalizing raw data so it can be effectively parsed by ML algorithms.

8. **Question:** Define Model Evaluation.
**Answer:** The process of using metrics and unseen testing data to determine how well a trained ML model generalizes.

9. **Question:** What does it mean to tie machine learning methods to outcomes?
**Answer:** The practice of ensuring the mathematical output of an ML model translates directly into a measurable, real-world result.

10. **Question:** What is the business need application of Machine Learning?
**Answer:** Leveraging ML to solve specific corporate problems, such as automating tasks, forecasting demand, or personalizing recommendations.
